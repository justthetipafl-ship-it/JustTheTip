ℹ️ App Guide
Everything you need to know to use Just The Tip
🎯 Matchup (MU) Ratings
How soft or tough a team is to score against, compared to the league average. A positive % means they concede more of that stat than average — good for your bet.
🟢
Soft
15%+ above avg — back it
🟡
Fav
5–15% above avg — favourable
⚪
Neutral
Within 5% — no real edge
🟠
Tough
5–15% below avg — be cautious
🔴
V.Tough
15%+ below avg — avoid
🏷️ Player Badges
Small badges appear next to player names throughout the app to give you instant context.
🚑 5-7 weeksInjured
On the injury list — hover for type. Red = out, amber TEST = game-time decision.
🚫 OMTOmitted
Named out or dropped — don't bet.
🏉 3Match FGS
Kicked the first goal of the match N times this season.
🐷 2Disposal King
Led the entire match in disposals N times this season.
🔍 Players Scanner
Reading the table
Stats marked $ are bettable markets. Click any column header to sort.

Green cell = soft matchup · D = favourable DVP
↑ trending up · ↓ trending down

The Pos column is a dropdown — click to override a player's position. A small ✎ appears when overridden. Changes persist across sessions and affect DVP, matchup ratings, and all suggestions.
Filters
Filter by team, position, opponent, games played, and time on ground.

↑ Above pos avg — only players beating their position group average for the current sort stat.
🏟️ Teams
All 18 teams ranked across 30+ stats organised into categories: Scoring, Disposals, Marks, Contested, Clearances, Hit Outs, Defence, Fantasy. Click a category pill to switch — the table auto-sorts by the first column in that group.

Toggle between For + Ag, For, or Against in the top-right. Green = above average, red = below. Click any team name to open the Team Modal — full attack stats, what they concede, DVP breakdown by position, and top stat leaders.
📅 Fixture
Game Cards
Each card shows matchup ratings and DVP by position group. Switch stats via the dropdown.
🔍 Focus This Game
Expands the full game panel — both squads side by side with matchup ratings, streakers, best FGS pick, top Multi Builder legs, and most disposals pick.
Squad List & Exclusions
Tap any player chip to exclude them from all suggestions for the session — their chip turns red. An ↺ Reset (N) button restores the whole team.
🛡️ DVP — Defence vs Position
More specific than overall matchup ratings — shows what each team concedes to each position group per stat. A team can be soft on Midfielder disposals but tight on Key Forward goals simultaneously.

Use the Play Style filter to narrow to a specific player archetype — it auto-sets the position and shows a player list below the team table with each player's DVP matchup this round.

Select a position to see the full ranked table. #1 = easiest matchup, #18 = toughest. Green = soft, red = hard.
🔎 Check My Bet
Type a player, stat, and line in any order: "Cripps 28+ disposals", "Neale clearances 5", "McKay 2 goals". Opponent is automatically factored in if a fixture is set.
What you get
Key stats — season avg, L5, hit rate, season high/low, DVP%, last game vs opponent.

Analysis factors — every signal explained: ✅ positive, ❌ negative.

Bar chart — last 20 games with dashed line marker. Amber = games vs this week's opponent.

Similar players — matched by playstyle, actual scores vs this opponent, ✓/✗ hit indicators.

📷 Save Image — exports the card as a PNG to share.
Verdicts
Strong Lean OVER — multiple signals align
Lean OVER — more positive than negative
No Clear Edge — mixed signals
Lean UNDER — more negatives
Strong Lean UNDER — avoid

✅ Degen Crew
Nine specialised tools for picking bets. Select a sub-page from the bar below the nav.
🎯 Multi Builder
Standard — conservative lines with high hit rates. Each leg scored on hit rate, form, matchup, consistency, and DVP. Click any leg to add it to your multi — combined odds shown at the bottom.

★ Full Round — one best leg per game across the entire round. Game tabs use abbreviated team names to fit more on screen.
🎯 Longshot — stretch lines for bigger odds. Only shown with a positive matchup signal. Hit rates 30–55%.

TAB Missed By 1 — filters to TAB's most common disposal markets (14, 15, 19, 20, 24, 25, 29, 30).
🏉 Snags
Top-10 goal kickers and forward stat leaders. Hot Matchups highlights a top-10 player facing a top-5 most permissive team this round.
⚡ Elite
Top-10 leaders for every tracked stat. Hot Matchups uses position-aware DVP — it checks if the opponent is a top-5 team allowing that stat to that player's specific position group.

🏉 First Goal Scorer
By Fixture — top FGS pick per game and per team, scored on Match FGS rate, Team FGS rate, first shot rate, goals avg, and context signals.
All Players — sortable table with Match FGS count/%, Team FGS count/%, 1st Shot count, 1st Shot Conv% (how often they convert when taking the first shot of the match), and avg time of first goal.
📊 Most Disposals
By Fixture — who's most likely to lead their team and the match in disposals. Scored on season avg, L5, DVP, opponent's disposal allowance, and H2H history.
All Players — ranked by match leader count, team leader count, season high, and average. The 🐷N badge goes to genuine match leaders only.
💀 Death Riders
Under disposals candidates — only Strong Lean UNDER picks. Scored on hit rate, avg gap, L5, L10, H2H history vs the opponent, DVP, overall matchup, and context signals.

Each card has a − / line / + adjuster to dial in the exact bookmaker line. The card disappears if adjusting the line pushes it out of Strong Lean UNDER territory.
🐰 Bunnies
Players who average more against their current opponent than any other team — minimum 3 games of H2H history. Covers Disposals (15+ avg), Fantasy (60+ avg), and Goals (1.5+ avg). Only shown when improvement is 10%+ over the next best average. Each card shows every previous score vs this opponent as coloured pills.
😬 Bogey
Players who have never covered their last 3 seasons average against this week's opponent — minimum 3 games of H2H history required. Covers Disposals and Goals. Shows every previous score as red pills and how far below their avg they typically go. Sorted by biggest gap first.
